# Design System: Paper Development Workshop on Data Studies

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "White Paper, Green Deadline"**

The site reads like a call for papers from the host institute (IWI-HSG), set with the discipline of anduril.com and palantir.com: one humanist face, hierarchy by scale instead of decoration, hairline index rows instead of cards, and colour only as large flat fields. The ground is white; the one loud object is the HSG-green deadline tile with the IWI diagonal edge. Everything else is ink, grey and rules.

Density is calm and editorial: a 12-column grid, generous section padding, light-weight display type at large sizes, and figures (dates, times, IDs) set in tabular lining numerals. Depth comes only from flat colour fields (white, light grey band, dark green, HSG green), never from shadows or blur. Corners are square throughout.

Motion is a layer on top of a page that is complete without it. It only draws rules and diagonals, moves type, fills colour and assembles square data points; it never introduces a new material. Self-started motion is confined to the first seconds after load and then stops; everything after that answers the visitor's scroll or pointer.

The earlier "Sovereignty Radar" look (dark ground, glows, glass cards, pill buttons) is retired and is not a reference.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White ground, ink text, HSG green reserved for actions, the deadline and key figures.
- Full-bleed flat colour fields as section changes: light grey band, the dark green ticker band, dark green faculty and footer, HSG green submit band; faculty and submit open with the IWI cut.
- One face (Source Sans 3) in three weights: 300 display, 400 text, 600 labels.
- Hairline rows (1px) for lists, dates, programme, faculty and tables; no cards.
- Square corners, no shadows, no blur; flat at rest.
- A focal hero sequence, then a continuous calm idle flow in the hero field and a running ticker band; everything else is driven by scroll and pointer. One page-wide pause control (keyboard-focus only) stops all self-running motion (WCAG 2.2.2).

## Colors

IWI institutional colours on white: one saturated green for action, one deep green for large quiet fields, greys for structure.

### Primary
- **HSG Green**: every action (primary buttons, links, required marks, focus rings, caret, selection), the deadline tile, the submit band, the receipt, the key date in the dates list, the programme progress line and the row-sweep line on white. White text on it and it on white both pass AA.

### Secondary
- **IWI Dark Green**: large quiet fields only: the ticker band, the faculty section, the mobile menu sheet, the open menu toggle, the footer. Also the hover fill of green and light buttons, and the faint pointer links in the hero data field.
- **Mint**: secondary text on dark green only (affiliations, footer labels, section facts on dark green), the hover colour of links on dark green, the square separators in the ticker band and the row-sweep line on the faculty rows.
- **Deep Rule Green**: hairlines on dark green.

### Neutral
- **Paper**: page ground, form panel, light buttons, input fields.
- **Band Grey**: the alternate section band (dates), programme break rows, utility bar, disabled buttons.
- **Ink**: all text and headings; the strong 1px rule on top of section heads and the agenda; outline buttons and the menu toggle border; the filled state of statement words and passed programme times.
- **IWI Grey**: secondary text (labels, hints, times, TBA), the second sentence of the hero statement, the unfilled state of statement words, input borders, and the closed-state deadline tile.
- **Rule Grey**: decorative hairlines between rows, and the outer, lightest points of the hero data field. Never text.
- **Draft Beige**: the draft bar and warning notes, always with ink text.
- **Danger Red**: field errors and error notes, on white only.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Field Rule.** Colour appears as large flat fields or as the colour of an action. Never as a tint, glow, gradient or accent border at rest. The only exception is the pointer light (see Motion System), which exists only under a fine pointer and is capped so text contrast never drops.

**The Green Means Go Rule.** HSG green marks what the visitor can do or must not miss (actions, the deadline, progress through the programme). Decorative green is dark green, in full-bleed fields.

**The Hairline-Is-Not-Text Rule.** Rule grey is for hairlines and data points only; text, hints and control borders that must be seen use IWI grey or ink.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Source Sans 3 (self-hosted, OFL; fallback Gill Sans, system sans)
**Body Font:** Source Sans 3
**Label/Mono Font:** Source Sans 3 600 for labels; no monospace except the admin debug block.

**Character:** A humanist sans standing in for IWI's Gill Sans; light 300 at display sizes gives the cool, technical register, 400 carries reading, 600 marks labels and field names.

### Hierarchy
Large roles (all 300, fluid):
- **Display** (clamp 44px to 96px, line-height 1, -0.025em): the hero title only.
- **Page Title** (clamp 40px to 72px, 1.05): inner page titles.
- **Tile Figure** (clamp 80px to 144px, 0.9, -0.03em, tabular): the deadline date in the tile only; a tile-only exception to the 96px display ceiling.
- **Figure** (clamp 40px to 64px, 1, tabular): dates in the dates list. The receipt ID runs clamp 40px to 72px at +0.04em; admin stat values 40px.
- **Headline** (clamp 32px to 56px, 1.1): section heads. Turns 400 below 720px so it holds on small screens.
- **Statement** (clamp 28px to 54px, 1.2, -0.015em, max 1120px): the large about statement that fills word by word.
- **Name** (clamp 26px to 40px, 1.15): faculty names and track names; 24px below 720px.
- **Claim** (clamp 26px to 36px, 1.2): the footer claim.
- **Ticker** (clamp 22px to 34px, 1.2): the running band.
- **Countdown** (clamp 28px to 32px, 1.15, tabular): countdown figures in the tile; 28px below 720px.

Text roles (fixed steps; the complete set is 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 22, 24 and 26px):
- **Hero Statement** (400, clamp 20px to 28px, 1.35, max 40ch).
- **Title Large** (400, 26px, 1.25): prose sub-heads on inner pages.
- **Title** (400, 24px, 1.25): sub-heads, hero date line, tile weekday, menu sheet links (300). Steps to 20px or 18px below 720px where noted in the build.
- **Title Small** (400, 22px, 1.3): agenda titles and date descriptions; 20px below 720px.
- **Lead** (400, 20px, 1.55, max 60ch): introductory paragraphs and the tile label; 18px below 720px.
- **Control** (400, 18px, 1.2): buttons, arrow links, section facts, faculty affiliation and role, form intro.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.6): running text, nav links, tile time; prose at max 68ch.
- **Body Small** (400, 16px, 1.5): field labels (600), checkbox text, bios, agenda details, receipt hint, footer claim line, and the mobile step of 18px text.
- **Small** (400, 15px, 1.4): hints, field errors (600), draft and utility bars, tables, footer base line, track notes.
- **Caption** (400, 14px): countdown unit letters, and the mobile step of the draft bar and slot names. Nothing smaller than 13px.
- **Label** (600, 13px, 0.06em, uppercase): keys in definition rows, footer column headings, status chips; the admin tag uses 13px 400. Never placed above a heading.

### Named Rules
**The Scale Not Effects Rule.** Hierarchy comes from size and the 300/400/600 weight steps. No gradient text, no outlines, no shadows on type. Motion may reveal or fill type; it never decorates it.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every date, time, count and ID uses lining tabular numerals, including the countdown.

**The Closed Ramp Rule.** New text uses a step from the ramp above; a size that is not listed is a defect, not a new step.

## Layout

A 12-column grid (24px gap; 16px below 720px) inside a 1440px max container with a 32px gutter (24px below 1024px, 16px below 720px). Sections are separated by 112px vertical padding (88px below 1024px, 56px below 720px) and by full-bleed colour changes rather than borders.

Every section opens with a section head: a 1px ink rule across the full width (white on green fields), the headline in columns 1 to 6 and a short grey fact in columns 7 to 12, then 56px of space (32px below 720px). Content below uses a main/side split (columns 1 to 6 and 8 to 12) or full-width index rows. The hero puts copy in columns 1 to 7 and the deadline tile in 8 to 12, bottom-aligned, with the data field drawn behind the whole hero. A dark green ticker band (20px vertical padding) sits directly under the hero. Fields that open with the IWI cut add the cut height to their top padding.

The sticky header is 72px (60px below 720px): logo, a ruled project name, five section links and a small green call to action. Below 1200px the links move into a disclosure menu; below 720px the project name hides and the call to action shortens to "Submit"; below 360px it hides. Everything collapses to a single column below 1024px, and index rows drop their key column below 720px.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. There are no shadows and no blur anywhere, and no gradients at rest. Depth is conveyed by tonal fields: white paper, the light grey band, dark green and HSG green. Layering is limited to the sticky header (white with a 1px rule beneath), the mobile menu sheet (dark green, dropping from the header) and the hero data field, which sits behind the hero content and never under a line of text.

### Named Rules
**The No Shadow Rule.** Surfaces are flat at rest and in every state. State is shown by fill, colour, underline or a drawn line, never by lift or shadow.

## Shapes

Square corners everywhere (0 radius), including buttons, inputs, the menu toggle, panels, chips, the programme marker, ticker separators and the data-field points. Borders are 1px: rule grey between rows, ink for section heads, the agenda top and outline controls, white above the countdown; 2px only for error states, the nav hover underline, the row-sweep line and the programme progress line.

The signature geometry is the **IWI cut**: a diagonal top edge of about 6.5 degrees, rising from left to right, made by clipping a flat green field. It appears on the deadline tile (64px rise; 40px below 720px) and across the full-width faculty and submit fields (min(11vw, 160px) rise). The menu sheet's opening wipe uses the same angle as its moving edge. It is never used on white or grey surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
Square, flat and direct, with an arrow that slides 4px right on hover and focus.
- **Shape:** square (0px), minimum 48px high, 12px 20px padding, 18px regular text; small variant 44px high, 17px.
- **Primary:** HSG green fill, white text; hover fills dark green.
- **Light:** white fill, green text, used on green and dark green fields; hover fills dark green with white text (mint on the menu sheet).
- **Line:** transparent with a 1px ink border; hover fills ink.
- **Disabled:** band grey fill, grey text, no arrow motion.
- **Focus:** 3px HSG green outline at 3px offset; white on green and dark green fields.
- **Magnetic:** the header call to action and the tile button follow a fine pointer by up to 8px and press to 97%.
- Inside the deadline tile and menu sheet the button runs full width with the label left and the arrow right.

### Arrow Links
Ink text at 18px with a 1px ink underline border and a trailing arrow; hover turns text and border green. Used for in-page jumps and external destinations. Plain body links are green, underlined 1px at 0.2em offset, turning ink on hover.

### Chips
- **Style:** 1px border in the current text colour, 13px 600 uppercase at 0.06em, no fill. Used for status such as "Provisional". The admin tag is a quieter 13px regular variant in grey (red for warnings).

### Cards / Containers
There are no cards. Lists of facts, dates, sessions, tracks and people are **index rows**: full-width rows divided by 1px hairlines, with an optional fixed key column (140px for definition keys, 180px for agenda times). On hover a 2px line sweeps along the row's bottom edge (green; mint on faculty) and the first cell moves 6px right.
- **Form panel:** white, 40px padding (20px 16px on mobile), sitting on the green submit band. In e-mail mode it holds the instructions instead: the address set large (`.mailbox`, light 300, clamp 24–40px, green link), a primary "Write the e-mail" button and an index of deadline, attachment, subject and e-mail contents.
- **Receipt:** HSG green block, 32px 40px padding, with the submission ID set as a large light figure.
- **Notes:** 1px grey border on white with a 20px stroked icon; success uses a green border, warning a beige fill, error a 2px red border. No coloured side stripes.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 1px IWI grey border, white fill, square, 48px minimum height, 17px text, green caret; textareas start at 160px. Labels sit above in 16px 600; hints below in 15px grey.
- **Hover:** border turns ink.
- **Focus:** green border plus a 2px green outline at 0 offset.
- **Error:** 2px red border and a 15px 600 red message below.
- **File field:** with script, a dashed grey frame holding an outline "Choose PDF" button and the file name; solid green frame once a file is chosen. Without script it stays the native control.
- **Checkboxes:** native, 22px, green accent colour.

### Navigation
Section links in 17px ink with a 2px transparent bottom border that turns green on hover. The mobile menu is a native disclosure: a 48px square toggle with a 1px ink border that fills dark green when open; the sheet is a dark green field of 24px light links on deep-green hairlines, each with a trailing arrow, ending in a full-width light button. Where view transitions are supported the sheet opens and closes as a wipe whose lower edge is the IWI diagonal; elsewhere it falls back to a straight clip-path wipe from the top.

### Deadline Tile
The signature component. An HSG green block under the IWI cut, holding the label (with a "Provisional" chip while the deadline is unconfirmed), the weekday, the date as the tile figure, the time with zone, the countdown row and a full-width light button. When closed it turns IWI grey, drops the countdown and the button, and gets no pointer light.

### Countdown
A row of three equal columns (days, hours, minutes) under a 1px white rule inside the tile: light tabular figures with 14px unit letters, spoken as full words for assistive technology. No seconds. It ticks once a minute against the deadline itself; a changed digit rolls up out of a clip box. When the deadline passes, the script turns the tile into its closed state. Without script it shows the server-rendered time left; it is hidden in print.

### Ticker Band
A full-bleed dark green band under the hero carrying facts the hero does not already state (never the title) as light 300 text separated by 10px mint squares. Decorative and hidden from assistive technology; it runs right to left at about 40px/s, speeds up while the visitor scrolls down, pauses on hover and with the page-wide pause control, and is static without script, under reduced motion and in print (hidden).

### Faculty Rows
Full-bleed dark green field opening with the IWI cut, with hairline rows in deep rule green: name large and light in columns 1 to 6, affiliation and role in mint to the right.

### Motion System
One easing family, motion only where it carries meaning, and nothing that runs on its own after the first seconds.

**Focal hero sequence (on load, once).** The title rises out of a mask (1s); date line, statement and links follow by 16px and fade in at 0.15s, 0.25s and 0.35s (0.8s each); the tile's IWI cut draws into the already-visible tile (1.1s after 0.2s). The data field fades in (600ms) as square points in HSG green, dark green and rule grey fly in from the left (staggered left to right over about 0.55s, 0.9s to 1.25s each) and settle into a ribbon that continues the tile's diagonal across the hero. Drift and breathing then ease to a stop over 1s and the frame loop parks.

**Scroll-linked (moves only while the visitor scrolls).**
- Statement fill: words turn from IWI grey to ink in reading order between 85% and 45% of the viewport (0.25s per word).
- Section heads: the rule draws from the left (1.2s), the heading rises out of a mask (0.9s, +80ms), the fact follows (+200ms). One-shot.
- Dates: rows rise 16px in turn, 80ms apart, each hairline drawing in after its row. One-shot.
- IWI cut on the faculty and submit fields: drawn from flat to full as the field's top travels from the bottom of the viewport to 55%, by scaling a paper-coloured triangle rather than re-clipping the field.
- Programme: from 1024px up, a 2px HSG green line with a 6px square marker grows down the left gutter (24px left of the agenda, clear of the break rows' 16px bleed); times of passed rows turn ink. Below 1024px there is no line; the times turning ink carry the progress.
- Ticker band: runs at 40px/s, scroll boost up to 4x (decays over about 0.5s), brief reverse when scrolling up; pauses on hover and with the pause control.

**Pointer-driven (fine, hovering pointer only).** The hero field pushes points aside within 120px and draws up to eight faint dark-green links, then springs back and parks. Magnetic buttons, the row sweep, the arrow nudge, and the pointer light: a 280px white radial light under the content of green fields, alpha capped at 0.05 on HSG green and 0.07 on dark green so white text keeps at least 4.5:1 and mint at least 5:1.

**Timing tokens.** Ease-out-expo `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for every movement; ease-out-cubic `cubic-bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)` for colour and opacity in scroll effects. Durations: 0.2s colour states, 0.3s arrow nudge and digit roll, 0.45s sheet wipe and row sweep (0.28s sheet close), 0.5s light fade, 0.6s to 1.2s reveals.

**The Settle-and-Stop Rule.** Anything that moves without the visitor asking stops by itself within about 4s of load and never restarts on its own (WCAG 2.2.2). Ongoing motion is allowed only when it is the direct result of scroll or pointer input; the countdown changes at most once a minute.

**The Content-First Rule.** Effects are progressive enhancement. Nothing visible at load is hidden or moved by script; elements are armed only while off-screen; the page is complete without any fx file.

**The Motion Policy (always on).** By decision of the project team (12.10.2026) the page ignores the visitor's prefers-reduced-motion setting: all animations always run. The only mechanism to stop self-running motion is the page-wide pause control (the running band's button, visible on keyboard focus only), which stops the hero idle flow and the band (WCAG 2.2.2). Known trade-off: visitors who turned animations off for medical reasons (vestibular disorders, migraine) still get motion; the earlier gentle tier is kept in the CSS as never-matching `@media not all` blocks and can be restored by reverting the media queries. Under forced colours the data field, programme line, row sweeps and pointer light are removed. In print, the field, ticker and countdown are hidden, cuts are removed and every animation is skipped.

**Performance rules.** Animate transform, opacity and colour only; never re-clip or repaint a full field on scroll. One requestAnimationFrame loop per module, reads before writes, idle when nothing moves; pause off-screen and in hidden tabs. Field capped at 720 points and a device pixel ratio of 2. will-change only on elements that are actively moving.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the ground white and let HSG green appear only as actions, the deadline tile, the submit band, the receipt, the key date and the programme progress line.
- **Do** separate sections with full-bleed flat fields (white, band grey, dark green, HSG green) and open each with the 1px ink section-head rule.
- **Do** list content as 1px hairline index rows with a key column, not as cards.
- **Do** set display and figures in Source Sans 3 300 and every date, time, count and ID in tabular lining numerals.
- **Do** take every text size from the ramp (13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26px and the listed fluid roles).
- **Do** keep every corner square and every control at least 44px high (48px for primary controls).
- **Do** use mint for secondary text on dark green and white for all text on HSG green.
- **Do** theme browser surfaces from the palette: green selection, green caret, green focus outline (white on green fields), grey-on-band scrollbar.
- **Do** draw icons as stroked SVG (1.75 stroke, square caps, miter joins, 24px grid, rendered 16px to 22px).
- **Do** keep self-running motion calm (hero idle flow, ticker), always pausable through the page-wide pause control (the band's button, visible on keyboard focus only, by decision of the project team; it toggles html.fx-paused and the fx:pause event), and remember that reduced-motion is deliberately ignored (see the Motion Policy).

### Don't:
- **Don't** use shadows, blur or glass on any surface or text, or gradients on any surface at rest.
- **Don't** raise the pointer light above 0.05 alpha on HSG green or 0.07 on dark green, or show it on the closed grey tile.
- **Don't** round corners or use pill buttons.
- **Don't** use rule grey for text or for borders that must be seen.
- **Don't** put text on beige other than ink, or red on anything but white.
- **Don't** apply the IWI cut to white or grey surfaces; it belongs to green fields.
- **Don't** add a small label or eyebrow above a heading; the heading and the grey section fact carry the context.
- **Don't** add looping or autoplaying motion, a seconds counter, or an effect that hides content until script runs.
